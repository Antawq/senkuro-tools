# SenkuTools Privacy Policy

SenkuTools does not collect, track, or send any user data.

## Data handling
- Settings: saved only in your browser (chrome.storage.local).
- Cookies: reads the Senkuro session cookie locally to show token details. Never sent to any server.
- Clipboard: copies a card name or poster image only when you click the copy button.

## Third parties
No servers, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Everything runs directly in your browser.
